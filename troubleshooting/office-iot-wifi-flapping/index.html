    Office A IoT Wi-Fi Flapping (fan-office-a, mini-switch-office-a)

    Investigated 2026-11-12. All times UTC (AP logs are CET, UTC+1). Neither device is resolved. The cerezo min-RSSI change did not stop the fan outages. Both devices were power-cycled on Nov 12 (~22:55Z); the 48 h verdict is pending, see Follow-up on 2026-11-14.

    Problem

    BlackboxProbeFailed fired repeatedly for fan-office-a (ICMP ping) and mini-switch-office-a (TCP 6053, the ESPHome API port). Config: system/monitoring/blackbox-exporter-values.yaml.

    • fan-office-a: 344 up/down changes in 7 d, outages of 35-243 min. Failures start 2026-11-03 ~13:07Z (probe-down count per day from ~0 to 100-558).
    • mini-switch-office-a: ~100% up until Nov 4, down from Nov 5 21:08Z, ~99% down since Nov 8 with brief recoveries (e.g. down Nov 12 09:25-20:41Z).
    • Only the Wi-Fi IoT devices in Office A flap (fan, mini switch, a couple of Shellys/plug). Wired and cluster targets are stable. Shelly/SU100 devices stay associated (1-3 drops in 15 d). SU100 devices fail 20-30 times/day since at least Oct 3: unrelated baseline, exclude them.

    Setup: two APs serve both IoT SSIDs, in or near the same room (UniFi controller 10.0.162).

    AP Model / fw 2.4 GHz Notes
    limonero U6-LR, 6.7.54 ch 6 auto, 20 MHz, power high ~17-23 clients, 29-36% busy, 18-29% TX retries, min-RSSI disabled, 139 d uptime
    cerezo BZ2, 4.3.28 (2.4 GHz only) ch 11, power medium ~1 client, min-RSSI -75 dBm since 21:32Z (was -67), ~26% TX retries

    fan-office-a is a Tuya ceiling-fan controller driven by localtuya (only 6668/tcp, no web UI or logs). mini-switch-office-a is an ESP8285 Athom mini switch (power_save_mode: none). It ran ESPHome 2025.10.2 without debug:; it now runs 2026.10.2 with debug: sensors (see below). Both devices are permanently mains-powered, with no relay dependency between them.

    Root Cause

    fan-office-a (causes found, but not all of them)

    1. cerezo min-RSSI kick. stamgr logged kick-sta ... reason:Low RSSI at 09:25:29Z (rssi 7) and 21:01:14Z (rssi 26) on Nov 12, both below the old threshold of 28 (-67 dBm). The 09:25 kick starts the 09:25-10:40Z outage. The fan's signal on cerezo fell from about -60..-62 dBm to -69..-71 dBm around Nov 2-3 (cause not found: physical move, TX power or interference are the candidates). Nov 3 13:59Z - Nov 4 18:20Z it went through 25 s-5 min drop/reconnect loops on cerezo, then sat on limonero at -80..-88 dBm (~34 drops there, ~59 UniFi drops total).
    2. Hourly group-key rekey on limonero. wpa_group_rekey=3600 runs at hh:01:2x for every client on the VAP. At 20:01:26Z the fan (silent since ~19:45Z, weak -85 dBm uplink) did not answer after 4 tries and the AP sent deauth reason 16. It had succeeded at 15:01-19:01Z. This explains the fixed hh:01:26 UniFi drop timestamps (12 since Nov 5). cerezo rekeys around hh:08:33.
    3. Silent-then-gone, no AP action (cause not visible). On cerezo the fan stayed associated ~47 min after its 21:05Z rejoin, the probe failed at 21:51Z, and at 21:52:43Z cerezo logged it leaving with no kick, no Low RSSI, no reason code and no provisioning event. So the Low-RSSI kicks are real but only one of several causes.

    mini-switch-office-a (not resolved)

    • UniFi disconnects happened at -46..-59 dBm, well above cerezo's threshold, and limonero has no min-RSSI, so this is not a signal kick.
    • limonero log at 20:59Z: 60 s keep-alive silence, then tx continue fail cnt = 200 (the AP cannot reach it). The mini sends its own deauth (reason 3) at 20:40:54Z and 21:01:13Z, coinciding with device-side reboots (~20:40:46Z and ~21:01:15Z).
    • 21:24-21:26Z outage, AP view (limonero). 21:24:07Z last frame, 21:25:07Z keep-alive silent for 60-sec, 21:25:51Z the AP removed it (MacTableMaintenance had left, per_err_times=61, tx continue fail cnt=69, DEAUTH reason 3 sent by the AP). No kick, no stamgr action, no provisioning. It never tried to associate on either AP afterwards.
    • Radio: ~12.6% TX retries, AP-to-device rate 1 Mbps, device-reported RSSI -75..-79 dBm vs UniFi -63..-65. Offline gaps per UniFi events: ~35 h, ~3.4 d, ~1.6 d, ~11 h (Nov 12 09:26-20:41); first drop Nov 2 19:24Z.
    • After the owner's flash the mini came back, then was down again from 22:26Z (the fan recovered at ~22:38Z): an AP restart did not bring it back.
    • Interpretation: the ESP radio hangs and the device reboots itself. wifi.reboot_timeout is 24 h (apps/esphome/config/packages/connection.yaml) but api.reboot_timeout is not overridden, so ESPHome's default of 15 min is the likely trigger (not verified). The reset reason was not logged before the flash.
    • The probe targets the API port, so it cannot tell a hung API from lost Wi-Fi. There is no ping probe for this device.

    Common pattern

    For the mini (21:24-21:26Z on limonero) and the fan (21:51-21:52Z on cerezo), the AP only sees the station go silent and get removed. The cause is not visible from the APs: radio hang, firmware or power.

    Fix / Workaround

    Applied 2026-11-12 (live change outside git, owner approved)

    On cerezo's 2.4 GHz radio only, Minimum RSSI was lowered from -67 dBm to -75 dBm (rssi 28 -> 20 at ~-95 dBm noise) at 21:32:53Z. Provisioning took ~45 s (21:33:04-21:33:47Z), no reboot, all clients stayed. The UniFi UI slider is in dBm (-67..-90). The save also added assisted_roaming_enabled: false as a default. No other AP or SSID setting changed.

    Result

    • Interim: it did not stop the outages. The fan stayed on cerezo ~47 min through the change, then dropped at 21:51-21:52Z with no kick (see cause 3).
    • Final result: to be filled in after the 48 h check, see Follow-up on 2026-11-14.

    ESPHome upgrade (mini switch), flashed 2026-11-12

    Flashed 2026.10.2 with debug: sensors (reset reason, device info, heap free, heap max block, loop time; ~516 B RAM, ~5.3 KB flash) to record why it reboots. The sensors are readable only while the device is reachable.

    The upgrade alone is not expected to fix it (~20-30%, low confidence):

    • Same Arduino ESP8266 core 3.1.2 and Wi-Fi blob in 2025.10.2 and 2026.10.x; no release note from 2025.10.2 to 2026.10.1 describes an ESP8266 Wi-Fi hang fix (PR 18333 only fixes an mDNS crash).
    • Unresolved upstream reports of the same symptom: esphome issues 16724 (API dropped while ping still works) and 14999 (API handshake timeout, weak Wi-Fi), ratgdo issue 541 (off Wi-Fi for hours, helped by wifi: phy_mode: 11G, available since 2026.5.0).
    • Fleet comparison: the two ESP8285 smart plugs run 2026.10.x on the identical Wi-Fi config and one is stable (0 flaps in 7 d), so the same firmware can hold Wi-Fi.

    Not done, optional: phy_mode: 11G, an api: reboot_timeout tweak.

    RF environment and the channel/power decision

    Read-only from the controller. No scan was stored: a spectrum scan disrupts the AP's clients, so it was not run.

    • stat/spectrum-scan is empty for both APs. Neighbour data exists only on each AP's own channel: limonero ch6 sees ~309 neighbour BSSIDs (298 weaker than 10 dB SNR, only 4 at 20 dB or more); cerezo ch11 sees 21 (all weak, strongest 25 dB SNR) plus 7 on ch10. Noise floor -96 dBm on both.
    • Channel busy: limonero ~29-30% (17 clients, 29% TX retries); cerezo 5-21% depending on the panel, TX retries ~26%, 1 client. About 14 points of the 21% are neither its own rx nor tx: non-Wi-Fi energy, matching the rx_clear=99/100, rx_frame=0 log lines.

    Decision (owner agreed): do not change cerezo's channel or TX power.

    • AP TX power only improves what the devices hear (downlink); the evidence is stations going silent and a weak uplink (fan ~-70 dBm at cerezo). limonero is already at high power and the mini still dropped there.
    • ch1 would be the only channel clear of limonero's ch6, but nothing measured supports it and retries are equally bad on both channels.
    • If revisited: do a planned 2.4 GHz scan on cerezo with the owner's go-ahead, and pin limonero to ch6 first (auto channel could land on 1 or 11).

    Related changes

    • ESPHome flash tooling (make flash-device, flash-device-migrate, device-logs, one ESPHOME_VERSION pin synced with the dashboard image by Renovate): apps/esphome/config/README.md.
    • Fleet hardening (OTA encryption reusing the API key, digest auth on the web servers), tracked in planning/esphome-security-hardening-todo.md; flashed on 2026-11-12 except offline devices. Anything holding the API key can flash devices.

    Follow-up on 2026-11-14

    Run it at ~22:50Z on Nov 14 or later (~48 h after the power-cycle, ~49 h after the cerezo change). Everything is read-only. Do not reboot an AP or device before the AP logs are copied.

    Baseline at the start of the window

    • Power-cycle: the owner power-cycled both devices at ~22:52-22:55Z on Nov 12. The mini booted at ~22:54:44Z with Reset Reason External System (the power-cycle itself, so it says nothing about earlier hangs), ESPHome 2026.10.2 with debug sensors. It associated on limonero at ~-63 dBm AP-side / -69 dBm device-side, ~2 % retries, no disconnects in a 90 s log capture.
    • Fan: back at ~22:55Z, probably on cerezo; limonero still held a silent stale entry for it at -84..-88 dBm.
    • Settings in effect: cerezo 2.4 GHz min RSSI -75 dBm since Nov 12 21:32:53Z (no channel/power change; cerezo rebooted manually at ~22:34:10Z). ESPHome fleet on 2026.10.2, OTA encryption required.
    • Probe state changes in the 7 d before (compare a 48 h count x 3.5):
    Target Changes / 7 d Note
    fan-office-a 348 outages of 35-243 min
    mini-switch-office-a 21 ~99 % down since Nov 8
    shellyplus2pm-office-a / -office-t 78 / 82 same-room comparison
    shellypmmini-z800 / smart-plug-v2-snapmaker 176 / 94 comparison

    Exclude the SU100 devices (~150 each, baseline since Oct 3).

    Checks

    a. Prometheus (datasource prometheus). The blackbox pod may have been replaced, so aggregate by target:

    sum by (target) (changes(probe_success{job="blackbox-exporter",target=~"fan-office-a|mini-switch-office-a|shellyplus2pm-office-a|shellyplus2pm-office-t"}[48h]))
max by (target) (min_over_time(probe_success{job="blackbox-exporter",target=~"fan-office-a|mini-switch-office-a"}[5m]))

    Run the second as a 48 h range query, step 5m: each 0 is a 5-minute-down window, 2 or more consecutive zeros is an outage longer than 5 min. Count the windows and the longest run. b. Mini in Home Assistant: the Reset Reason, Device Info, Heap Free, Heap Max Block and Loop Time sensors and their last-changed times. A changed Reset Reason means it rebooted by itself: Software/System restart fits the 15 min API timeout, a watchdog/exception reason fits a firmware hang, External System or Power-on fits a power event. Uptime: cd apps/esphome/config && timeout 90 make device-logs DEVICE=mini-switch-office-a. c. APs over SSH, before anything is rebooted (RAM-only logs, limonero keeps ~7 h): ssh cerezo / ssh limonero (key auth, read-only commands such as cat /proc/uptime, grep, wlanconfig). In /var/log/messages and messages.old look for Low RSSI kicks, group key handshake failed, tx continue fail and DEAUTH reason codes per client (3 / 16 / 2). Note which AP each client is on. d. Fan in Loki (namespace home-assistant, 48 h): localtuya disconnect lines for the fan, e.g. {namespace="home-assistant"} |~ "(?i)localtuya" |~ "(?i)disconnect|lost|timeout" (adjust to the real message text, which was not checked). e. UniFi client view for the two devices (AP, signal, retries), through the firefox-devtools MCP, API prefix /api/s/default (see How to Diagnose for hygiene).

    Decision

    Success criteria against the baselines above:

    Outcome Criteria over 48 h
    Fixed both devices up, at most a handful of transitions each, no outage longer than 5 min
    Partially fan or mini improved by more than 50 % (changes, or down-time share for the mini) but still dropping
    Not fixed anything else

    Actions by evidence (one change at a time):

    Evidence Action
    Mini hangs again, no self-reboot, Reset Reason unchanged Hardware/firmware: replace the mini switch (an ESP32-based one is the obvious swap), or try wifi: phy_mode: 11G first. Add a ping probe in system/monitoring/blackbox-exporter-values.yaml to separate a hung API from lost Wi-Fi
    Mini self-reboots with Software/System restart The API-timeout reboot is firing: find out why HA/the probe lost the API (api: reboot_timeout default is 15 min; do not disable it)
    Fan still dropping with Low RSSI kicks Lower or disable cerezo min RSSI further, or put an AP closer
    Fan dropping with group-key failures on limonero Lengthen/disable the group rekey on the fan's IoT SSID only (owner approval, one SSID)
    Fan silent then gone, no AP reason Link margin is the problem: move/add an AP closer to the room's ceiling fixture, check the Tuya module position
    Nothing improves Consider a UniFi exporter (e.g. unpoller) for per-client RSSI/retries over time, and replacing cerezo (old 2.4 GHz-only BZ2; evidence that it is faulty is weak)

    Rules: no channel or TX-power change without a planned 2.4 GHz scan (see the decision above) and a pinned limonero channel; owner approval for any live UniFi change.

    How to Diagnose

    Prometheus (read-only):

    sum by (target) (changes(probe_success{job="blackbox-exporter"}[7d]))
min_over_time(probe_success{target=~"fan-office-a|mini-switch-office-a"}[1h])

    Then correlate the outage start with AP logs.

    • Collect AP logs first, and before rebooting an AP. AP logs are RAM-only: the manual cerezo reboot (~22:34:10Z; boot-log clock ~90 s early before NTP sync) destroyed the pre-reboot log. limonero keeps only ~7 h. SSH to the AP and copy /var/log/messages and messages.old (or logread).
    • Look in the AP logs for stamgr kick-sta ... reason:, wpa_group_rekey, MacTableMaintenance and DEAUTH reason codes 3/16/2. The controller event log has no disconnect reason (legacy stat/event returns empty); the AP logs do.
    • UniFi API. The prefix on this controller is /api/s/default/... (/proxy/network/... returns 404). stat/device has min_rssi_enabled/min_rssi (-67 dBm = rssi 28 at noise -95; -75 dBm = rssi 20). stat/rogueap lists neighbours only on the AP's own channel; stat/spectrum-scan is empty unless a scan was run.
    • Avoid iwpriv ra3 show stainfo on limonero: it segfaulted that process.
    • cerezo (BZ2, old dropbear) needs legacy SSH algorithms (ssh-rsa host/pubkey, group1 kex), set in the owner's ~/.ssh/config host entry: a plain modern client or a tool that ignores that config (paramiko, sshpass with default options) fails during key exchange.
    • Tool hygiene: controller tool output can expose the session cookie/CSRF token in request headers: treat it as sensitive and log out afterwards. The firefox-devtools MCP allows one WebDriver session.
    • Probing from the workstation does not work: it cannot reach the IoT VLAN (pings fail even for healthy Shellys). Use the blackbox exporter's view or a host on the VLAN.
    • Prometheus has no UniFi/AP metrics, so AP-side history exists only in RAM logs (hence the exporter idea in the Decision table).
    • Timezones: UniFi events UTC, AP logs CET, Home Assistant logs local.
    • Mini switch device logs: make device-logs DEVICE=mini-switch-office-a, see the runbook in apps/esphome/config/README.md (needs secrets.yaml from the esphome-secrets secret).

    Pitfalls / Dead Ends

    Do not repeat these:

    • Shared mains glitch or the Nov 3 battery/AC-failsafe work. No correlation: fan failures began 13:07Z, before the first Nov 3 evening commit at 20:51Z; failures do not cluster at hh:01; only the fan failed at 20:01 and 21:01 (no Shelly/SU100 dropped). There is no voltage or power series in Prometheus to check it directly.
    • Fan powered through the mini switch relay. Ruled out by the owner. The fan's Errno 111 (fresh boot) 4 s after the mini's 20:40:46Z reboot is a coincidence or a shared upstream cause.
    • An hh:01 job in the repo. Nothing runs at minute 01 (HA time_pattern every minute; battery-optimizer slots :00/:15/:30/:45). The hh:01 pattern is the WPA group rekey.
    • The blackbox exporter itself. Probe results match device-side evidence.
    • "cerezo is unhealthy because of its short uptime." Wrong: that was the manual reboot. The ~20k cumulative beacon-stuck/HAL resets since boot are unexplained but unproven harmful; pstore was empty after the reboot.
    • The cerezo reboot as the cause of the 22:26-22:35Z outage. Both devices were down from 22:26Z, before the ~22:34Z reboot (cause unknown).
    • AP-wide faults. No crash, watchdog or radio reset on limonero (139 d uptime, no channel changes, Radio AI disabled). cerezo logs frequent rx_clear=99/100 interference lines, but the reset counters did not move over 30 s and are not tied to incidents. The Nov 4 16:42-16:43Z event, where four clients left cerezo within seconds (one at -85 dBm), looks like a cerezo radio disturbance.
    • A failing probe does not prove the cause (the mini's hits the API port): check the AP kick/deauth reason first.
