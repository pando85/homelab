    v5-era room audio: replay false wakes and missed-wake mining (2026-11-11)

    Source: owner approval of 2026-11-11 (copy and score once). Follows the vnext ablations and its Next item 3 (fresh room exposure). Everything ran offline on the workstation; audio, scores and candidate windows stay in the private work directory (~/kivi-models/data/datasets/real/, data register entries 20 and 21 in the register). Nobody listened to, transcribed or printed any audio; this note holds counts, rates and scores only. Continued every day by the iteration loop (kivi_collect.py, from 2026-11-12).

    Day split (fixed by the owner, 2026-11-11)

    By Europe/Madrid local date, alternating: odd dates = TEST, even dates = MINE/TRAIN (2026-11-08 mine, 11-09 test, 11-10 mine, 11-11 test, and on alternately; pieces are cut at local midnight). The manifest split of the copied pieces is mine-v5era or test2-v5era. Test-day audio may be scored and labelled for evaluation, but is never used to choose models or thresholds. The rule lives in kivi_real_common.day_role; the live-wake split command follows the same rule.

    What was copied and scored

    • Copy: kivi_negatives.py --since 2026-11-08T13:28:10Z --until 2026-11-11T16:52:39Z --exclude-pipeline "Kiwi Test" --out-name negatives-v5era --part-test-day test2-v5era --part-mine-day mine-v5era. The period starts when both TVs ran the v5 wake word. The tool also drops each file's first 0.3 s and last 7 s, windows around presses and sessions, and 7 s after run starts that follow a detection within 60 s.
    • 47 pieces, 146.19 h, 16.84 GB: c3-a 72.80 h, c3-b 73.39 h; mine-v5era 66.70 h (7.68 GB), test2-v5era 79.49 h (9.16 GB). Hours per device and day (h): 11-08 mine 9.49 / 9.43, 11-09 test 23.00 / 23.81, 11-10 mine 23.94 / 23.84, 11-11 test 16.37 / 16.31 (c3-a / c3-b). negatives-v5era/COMPLETE.json marks the finished copy.
    • Scoring, once: kivi_replay_mine.py score, the server's streaming runtime (pyopen-wakeword, 1280-sample chunks, never batched) running v1 kivi, v4 oye_kivi and v5 oye_kivi_next together, uncalibrated files from kivi-models (sha256 in scores/v5era-replay/score_meta.json). Raw points: v4 0.9437, v5 0.9754, v1 0.98. Benchmark 300 s per worker with 6 workers: 66x real time (45.9x with one worker), estimate 2.33 h; the full pass took 124.3 min wall at 6 workers, nice 10, CPUQuota 600 %.
    • A piece is cut into 20 min ranges that run in parallel, each started 60 s early with its warm-up dropped. This is tested equal to the continuous stream (unit test on noise, and on a real 20 min range: 52 of 52 steps above 0.3 with identical times and a score difference of 0.0).
    • Per range only the steps where some model scored above 0.3 are kept. Events merge such steps within 1.5 s (33,253 events, events.csv). Detections use the server's rule (strictly above the raw point, 2 s refractory).
    • eval_stream.stream drops the remainder of the last 1280-sample chunk of a stream; this scorer has the same semantics (floor of whole chunks). On room audio that is a 1-chunk end effect per piece: noise, no bias.

    Reading: v5 fires 4 to 7 times less often than v4 on the same audio (the model change did what the ablations said), and c3-b is the noisier room. The per-cell counts are small (0 to 9), so the intervals overlap; the c3-b vs c3-a gap at v5 is not significant at 0.9754 (9 in 73.4 h against 2 in 72.8 h).

    Cross-check against live. The 27 live v5 detections (16 on c3-b, 11 on c3-a, live_wakes.csv) were in the dropped file tails, so the replay is not expected to re-find them. The replay fires 11 times in 146 h (0.075/h) against about 0.18/h live (27 in the same 146 h): lower, not higher, so no feature or level mismatch is indicated. A direct check scored the 6 s tails of the live v5 detections with the same runtime: the live model's peak is above its raw point in 13 of 27 tails and above 0.9 in 22 of 27 (median 0.971); a 6 s tail starts cold (no 8 s of context), so a lower peak than live is expected. Not conclusive per clip, and the v4-era tails (4 of 37 above the raw point) include runs that closed for other reasons, so they are not a check.

    (b) Missed-wake candidates

    Pool: events where v5 did not fire in the replay (peak at or below 0.9754) and v4 or v5 peaks at 0.5 or more (v1 alone is excluded: it has 12,372 events above 0.5). The pool has 1,734 events (mine c3-a 220, mine c3-b 613, test c3-a 267, test c3-b 634).

    Ranking (rank_score): v5 peak + 0.25 x v4 peak + 0.10 x v1 peak + retry evidence (a live detection or an stt-only (push-to-talk / mic-button) run on the same device within 30 s after the event +0.5; another pool event 5 to 30 s later +0.1). Retry evidence in the pool: live 23, stt-only 0 within the window, another event 574, none 1,137 (7 stt-only runs on c3-a and 32 on c3-b exist since the v5 era; one lies in the window of a selected event).

    Selection: at most 40, round-robin over part x device (10 each), highest rank first, at least 10 s apart on a device. The first round is 40 candidates: mine c3-a 10, mine c3-b 10, test c3-a 10, test c3-b 10; by retry evidence live 8, another event 28, none 4. v5 peaks of the 40 lie between 0.663 and 0.973; 12 have v4 at or above 0.9437. 1,694 more exist: left out with a v5 peak of 0.9 or more 93, of 0.8 or more 315, of 0.6 or more 881; the lowest rank taken is 1.195 (the best left out, 1.636, sits in a cell that already had its 10).

    Each candidate is a 6 s WAV window centred on the peak in datasets/real/live-misses/<part>/<device>/<UTC>/window.wav, listed in miss_candidates.csv (id, device, part, UTC, three peaks, retry evidence and detail, rank, empty label and speaker, level and peak dBFS). The owner labels them by listening, scores hidden:

    cd ~/homelab/scripts/voice-assistant/wakeword
~/kivi-models/data/.venv/bin/python kivi_live_wakes.py review --table miss_candidates.csv

    Labels: attempt (someone said "oye Kivi" / "Kivi" to it), other-speech, tv-media, noise, unclear; speaker spk01, spk02, spk-other, unknown (asked for attempt and other-speech). A window of a test day is labelled for evaluation only.

    The 11 replay detections of v5 at 0.9754 are not in this round (the pool excludes them by definition); they are the numerator of table (a) and could be a cheap second round if the owner wants them labelled.
